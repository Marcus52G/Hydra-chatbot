// Common Questions

Answers To What You're Thinking

How quickly can my bot go live?
Within 24 hours of signing up. We handle the full setup — you just share your menu/products and business details with us.
Do I need to buy any equipment or software?
No. You just need your existing WhatsApp Business number. We handle everything technical on our end.
What if a customer asks something the bot can't handle?
The bot politely notifies the customer and escalates to you directly. You stay in full control of edge cases.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — no long-term contracts. If you cancel, your bot stops at the end of the billing month. No penalties, no questions asked.
What's the real difference between Basic and Smart?
Basic: customers follow a set menu (press 1 for orders, 2 for prices). Smart: customers type naturally — "niambie bei ya chicken" — and the bot understands, responds, and remembers them next time.
Can I upgrade from Basic to Smart later?
Absolutely. Many clients start on Basic to experience automation, then upgrade to Smart once they see orders flowing in automatically.
